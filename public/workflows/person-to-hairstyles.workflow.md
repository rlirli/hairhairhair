# Person research to hairstyle records

Use this workflow for person research, profile-image discovery, appearance research, image-only hairstyle analysis, and optional hairstyle catalog follow-up. Image publication and temporary analysis are separate decisions.

## Orchestration

- Run each task in a fresh isolated subagent. Begin with profile research or appearance discovery according to the user’s scope; profile research may recommend discovery next. Follow up only when the orchestrator chooses to.
- Profile research searches for a candidate portrait as well as person facts. It reports portrait and rights evidence in findings; it does not download or persist image files. The candidate is research output only: a separate, explicitly authorized image workflow must approve and persist it before any public use.
- Discovery collects appearance, photo-source, and independent event/date evidence without consulting the hairstyle catalog or assessing reuse rights. Keep the photo URL distinct from the non-photo event/date source. Discovery does not download or persist images.
- Asset review makes independent `publishable`, `analysis-only`, `withhold`, or `needs-review` decisions. A paid or absent publication license does not by itself block temporary image-only analysis. Follow the jurisdiction and source-access checks in the asset-ingestion prompt; do not proceed when access or analysis is prohibited or materially unclear.
- For each image approved for analysis, whether `publishable` or `analysis-only`, run hair observation in a fresh isolated subagent with only the actual image and that task prompt. Exclude captions, filenames, source URLs, Appearance metadata, person profile, source claims, catalog data, and prior analysis. Merge its image-only observation fragment into the full Appearance in orchestrator state, preserving unrelated fields.
- Only after observation may the orchestrator invoke catalog matching in another fresh isolated subagent. Reconcile against the full local catalog before linking or proposing a stub. Public references are published-only and omit drafts and stubs. If no reliable match is possible, an absent `hairstyleId` is valid; it may be linked in a later run. In web mode, return proposed JSON and assets in chat, and do not claim repository writes.
- In local mode, use the content MCP attached to the active checkout. Use `content_add_image` and persistent Media records only for images approved for public reuse. For `analysis-only`, keep a working copy outside the repository/content tree, hand the image directly to the observer, and delete the temporary copy after observation. Never persist the photo URL, image file, or Media record for that analysis-only Appearance. Use an independent non-photo event/date source for a persisted Appearance's required `taken.sourceUrl`; if none is available, defer persistence. When bringing web-produced changes into the repository, reread complete records, merge only intended fields, preserve private data, and reconcile IDs. The orchestrator decides when to persist or commit before any dependent phase.
- For a `publishable` image, persist it only after the license/permission covers every intended public use at no monetary license cost; record attribution and other terms. For an `analysis-only` image, do not persist its photo URL in Appearance, Source, or Media records. If analysis is prohibited or needs review, do not transfer the image to the observer. Do not claim that omission from repository records erases browser, tool, or provider logs.
- Stub selection and guide research are optional follow-ups after matching. If authorized, researched styles may proceed through imagery and example creation: generate and register the PNG/media first, then pass that image and media record to the example task. The example task does not generate another image. Web mode returns the actual image attachment and proposed records.

## NEXT

The orchestrator may continue from profile research to appearance discovery, from discovery to asset review, or from an image approved for `publishable` or `analysis-only` use to observation and later catalog matching, according to scope and available records. `analysis-only` does not mean that the image or its photo URL is persisted. An `analysis-only` Appearance has no `imageId`; its required `taken.sourceUrl` points to independent non-photo event/date evidence. Stub research and style examples are separate optional follow-ups. Do not launch a follow-up unless instructed otherwise.
