# Will Smith photo research

Research date: 2026-09-21

## Rights and identity rule

The target is Willard Carroll “Will” Smith II (the actor, rapper, and producer), not the baseball, football, hockey, or political namesakes. For this feature, accept only an explicit public-domain basis (for example, a U.S. federal employee’s official-duty photograph) or an explicit CC0 dedication. Do not substitute CC BY, CC BY-SA, GFDL, “some rights reserved,” rights-managed, or royalty-free files. Copyright status does not settle publicity/personality rights or imply endorsement.

The event/capture date below is the date the photograph depicts or the file records as taken. Commons upload, Flickr publication, and mirror dates are separately noted where useful; they are not capture dates.

Do not fill the timeline with celebrity-event photographs merely because they are on Commons: the 1993 Emmy photographs by Alan Light are CC BY 2.0, the 1999 Baltimore photograph is CC BY-SA 2.0, the 2009 Nobel Peace Concert photographs are CC BY-SA 3.0, and the 2017 Cannes photograph is CC BY-SA 3.0. Those are rejected under the project rule.

## Official-source rights notes

The [DVIDS copyright notice](https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright) says official-duty U.S. government visual information is generally not copyrightable in the United States, while warning that some posted media can contain non-DoW rights and that publicity/privacy and endorsement restrictions remain. That policy supports the Navy federal-work basis; it does not turn a Getty or Flickr copy into a cleared original. The Commons original’s embedded Navy caption identifies the 23 May 2012 capture, MC2 Drae Parker, VIRIN `120523-N-MH374-101`, and the official release. The capture date is 23 May 2012; a 24 May publication or mirror date is not a substitute. Preserve the Commons file record, Navy caption, VIRIN, and public-domain Navy basis alongside the downloaded original. No direct DVIDS original is required for this selected asset.

The two official White House/NARA Obama-era files selected for the person-page asset set are `P121009PS-0547` (10 December 2009 Nobel Peace Prize event) and `P042411PS-0363.JPG` (24 April 2011 White House event). They are U.S. government/NARA collection images with public-domain federal-work provenance, supported by the [Obama Presidential Library finding aid](https://www.obamalibrary.gov/digital-research-room/finding-aids/photographs-will-smith-22-64503-f). This is distinct from the 11 December 2009 Oslo Nobel Peace Concert photographs above: those were private photographer Harry Wad’s CC BY-SA 3.0 works and remain rejected.

## Rejected candidates and why

- [Will Smith (2078379272), 1993 Emmy Awards](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Will_Smith_(2078379272).jpg>): genuine actor and exact capture date (19 September 1993), but CC BY 2.0; the page also identifies Alan Light and a Flickr source. Not public domain/CC0.
- [Will Smith.jpg, 1993 Emmy Awards](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Will_Smith.jpg): genuine actor and exact date, but CC BY 2.0; the permission text is attribution permission, not a PD or CC0 dedication.
- [Will Smith (32335923807), Baltimore 1999](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Will_Smith_(32335923807).jpg>): genuine actor and taken in 1999, but CC BY-SA 2.0.
- [Nobel Peace Prize Concert 2009](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nobel_Peace_Price_Concert_2009_Will_Smith_and_Jada_Pinkett_Smith_with_children2.jpg): genuine actor and exact 11 December 2009 date, but CC BY-SA 3.0 and photographer Harry Wad’s attribution/usage conditions.
- [Will Smith Cannes 2017](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Will_Smith_Cannes_2017_3.jpg): genuine actor and exact 17 May 2017 date, but CC BY-SA 3.0, plus Commons’ personality-rights warning.
- [Will Smith (51362604855), White House 2021](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Will_Smith_(51362604855)_(cropped).jpg>): public-domain federal-photo claim is accompanied by a White House Photo Office restriction, and the depicted Will Smith is the Los Angeles Dodgers catcher, not the actor.
- [Will Smith Saints](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Will_Smith_Saints.jpg): football player, not the actor; reject despite a U.S. government public-domain basis.
- [Will Smith hockey photos](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:William_Smith_Hokey_player.jpg): hockey player, not the actor; explicit CC0 but wrong identity. The 2026 practice image is also CC BY-SA 4.0 in another file.

## Editorial recommendation

Use exactly the three selected original files: NARA `P121009PS-0547` (10 December 2009), NARA `P042411PS-0363` (24 April 2011), and the uncropped Commons Navy `120523-N-MH374-101` (capture date 23 May 2012). All three have a documented public-domain U.S. government official-duty basis. Keep a visible credit and a separate note that public-domain copyright status does not grant a model/personality release or imply endorsement. Do not relabel CC BY/CC BY-SA celebrity photographs as public domain.
