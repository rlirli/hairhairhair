# Mario Balotelli hairstyle research

The Balotelli-inspired guides are general hairstyle records, not claims that the generated portraits depict Mario Balotelli. The person profile and appearance records remain the place for dated real-world evidence.

## Sources

- [Mario Balotelli’s hair: an obituary 2008–2015](https://www.theguardian.com/fashion/2015/aug/26/mario-balotellis-liverpool-milan-loan-hair-an-obituary), The Guardian, reviewed 2026-09-22. The overview documents Balotelli’s changing cuts, including mohawk and all-blond variations.
- [Mario Balotelli: Blond bombshell](https://www.mancity.com/news/first-team/first-team-news/archive/2011/november/mario-balotelli-blond-bombshell), Manchester City, reviewed 2026-09-22. The club archive provides a first-party source for a blond Balotelli hairstyle during his Manchester City period.

## Editorial decisions

- Patterned mohawk and thin mohawk are separate guides because the first is defined by side design while the second is defined by a narrow central strip.
- Dark and blond thin mohawks are variations of one guide, not separate hairstyle records.
- Cropped afro is distinct from the existing Buzz cut because it retains visible rounded texture and fuller shape. The existing Buzz cut remains the simpler uniform clipper option.
- Top knot is a separate styling-technique guide because its defining action is gathering and securing crown length; tapered and curly references are variations.
- All generated assets are fictional transparent RGBA references made with OpenAI’s built-in image generation tool. They carry generated provenance in the media registry and receive their light/dark background from the site’s CSS.
