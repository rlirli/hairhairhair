# Hairstyle imagery: visual direction

Each image is a single contemporary editorial portrait of a fictional adult. The hair is the clear subject: its silhouette, length, shape, texture, parting, and finish are easy to assess at a glance.

The background is genuinely transparent. The portrait is cleanly isolated, with natural hair strands and fine edges retained around the full hairstyle silhouette. No solid backdrop, studio wall, scenery, or baked-in shadow fills the transparent area.

The composition is a shoulder-up portrait with the entire hairstyle visible and breathing room around its outline. A front, profile, or three-quarter view makes the defining cut or styling detail legible. Lighting is soft and neutral, with natural-looking color, realistic skin texture, and restrained contrast.

Clothing is simple and quiet—often cream or another muted solid—so it does not compete with the hair. The image contains no celebrity likeness, text, logos, watermarks, jewelry, hats, or distracting props. It does not imply a precise natural hair subtype from appearance alone. The selected hairstyle is represented faithfully, without dramatic styling details that change its defining shape.

The image must have a genuinely transparent RGBA background with clean alpha edges; do not bake in a white, ivory, studio, or checkerboard background.
