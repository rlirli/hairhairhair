# Hairstyle imagery

**Task ID.** `hairstyle-imagery`

**Goal.** Create one original image that clearly represents a supplied, established hairstyle.

**Input.** A complete hairstyle concept or record and optional view or variation.

**Return.** The actual PNG and a standard [content task result](https://hairhairhair.hair/schemas/content-task-result.schema.json) with a proposed Media record and image asset entry. Report whether transparency is present and any limitations. Do not claim the image was saved or registered unless the execution environment confirms that separately.

**Image.** Follow [hairstyle imagery style](https://hairhairhair.hair/prompts/hairstyle-imagery-style.md): square, genuinely transparent background, shoulder-up portrait of a fictional adult, full hairstyle visible, no celebrity likeness or text. Preserve the defining cut or styling. Do not infer a natural hair subtype. Stop if the supplied concept is only a stub or does not identify a clear visual target. Do not create a style-example record.
